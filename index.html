About

Hi! I'm Eloise (Xitong He), a student at the University of Washington with an intended major in Statistics & Informatics, expected to graduate in 2029 (GPA 3.66).

I'm a data analyst at Solid Ground, a Seattle nonprofit, where I turn housing service records into insights and dashboards that help place people in homes. This past summer I worked as a research assistant in Prof. Zhi-Hua Zhou's LAMDA group at Nanjing University, building a benchmark for reusing machine-learning models.

I've also spent years teaching — calculus, statistics and TOEFL — and I enjoy explaining ideas clearly. Outside of data, I design clothing inspired by ethnic minority cultures, and one of my series was published in a French fashion magazine.
